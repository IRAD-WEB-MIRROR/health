Vaccination

Vaccinations of the National Immunisation Calendar are free at the polyclinics, maternity hospitals and health posts. Each child has a vaccination card (form 063/u) kept at the polyclinic, and a vaccination certificate for the parents. The certificate is needed for kindergarten and school.

When are my child's vaccinations?

Enter the date of birth. The dates are approximate; your polyclinic will invite you.

Seasonal influenza 2026-2027

Free from 1 October to 15 December 2026 for children from 6 months to 5 years, pregnant women, persons over 60 and pensioners, persons with chronic heart and lung disease and diabetes, medical workers, teachers and staff of children's homes. Others: 85 som at the polyclinic, while stocks last.

Mobile vaccination teams, November-December 2026

The teams vaccinate children who missed vaccinations, give the influenza vaccine to the groups above and vitamin A to children under 5. The village council and the health post announce the time of arrival.

Other vaccinations

  • Rabies: after an animal bite go to the rabies room of the nearest hospital the same day. Vaccine and immunoglobulin are free.
  • Before travel: vaccinations against yellow fever and other diseases for travel abroad are not given in the Zone; ask the polyclinic about the certificate of vaccinations received.
  • Brucellosis: livestock are vaccinated by the State Veterinary Inspectorate. People are not vaccinated: boil milk (advice).
