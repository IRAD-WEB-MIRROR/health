State pharmacies Nos. 1-60

The state pharmacies sell the medicines of the List of Essential Medicines at the prices approved by the Ministry (Order No. 241 of 01.09.2026), and dispense free medicines by prescription for the groups entitled to them. On the signs: Mamlekettik dariykana No. ... / Государственная аптека № ....

Prescription medicines are sold only on presentation of a prescription of the established form. Narcotic and psychotropic medicines: special prescription form, only in pharmacies Nos. 1, 5, 27, 38 and 49.
