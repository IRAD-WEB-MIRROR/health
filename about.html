About the Ministry

The Ministry of Health is the state body of the Irad Special Economic Zone that carries out state policy in health care and the sanitary and epidemiological welfare of the population. It is one of the ten ministries of the Zone and reports to the Lieutenant Governor, who chairs the cabinet.

History

The Ministry continues the Ministry of Health of the Iradi SSR and the territorial Department of Health (1992-2006), headed by a Secretary appointed by the Governor. In December 2006 the Department became the Ministry of Health; Executive Directive 2007/09 of 6 August 2007 confirmed it among the ministries of the Zone. The e-mail address of the Ministry keeps the territorial name: info@health.id.gov.

During the territorial period the main hospitals of Talas were rebuilt and re-equipped with United States federal grants and territorial loans: the Regional Hospital (1995-2002, with a new emergency and trauma center), the emergency department of Municipal Hospital No. 1 (2004). Residency programmes on the American model were introduced at the Faculty of Medicine of Talas State University.

Functions

  • management of the state hospitals, dispensaries and district hospitals; methodological guidance of the city hospitals and polyclinics, which are run by the city governments;
  • the rural health posts in the districts;
  • the State Sanitary and Epidemiological Service, the National Immunisation Calendar and the vaccination rounds, carried out together with the United Nations Mission in Irad;
  • the state pharmacies Nos. 1-60 and the List of Essential Medicines;
  • licensing of medical and pharmaceutical activity;
  • training of medical staff with the Faculty of Medicine of Talas State University.

Rural health posts

The Ministry runs 52 rural health posts (feldsher and midwife posts) in the districts. Many were repaired and equipped with the help of the Anara Toktarova Children's Fund.
