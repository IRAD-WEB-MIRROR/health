ORDER

14.08.2019     Talas     No. 112

On the National Immunisation Calendar

In accordance with the Act on the Sanitary and Epidemiological Welfare of the Population, for the purpose of protecting the population from infections preventable by vaccination,

I ORDER:

1. To approve the National Immunisation Calendar (annex 1).

2. To include from 1 January 2020 the vaccination against pneumococcal infection (PCV) and the inactivated polio vaccine (IPV) in the Calendar.

3. Heads of polyclinics, maternity hospitals, district hospitals and health posts shall ensure vaccination of children according to the Calendar, keep the vaccination cards (form 063/u) and report monthly to the State Sanitary and Epidemiological Service.

4. Vaccinations of the Calendar are carried out free of charge.

5. Refusal of vaccination is recorded in writing in the child's medical record, signed by a parent.

6. Control over the execution of this order is entrusted to the Chief State Sanitary Doctor.

Minister
