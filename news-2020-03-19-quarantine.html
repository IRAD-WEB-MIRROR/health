Quarantine regime: what to do if you have symptoms

By Executive Directive 2020/05 a quarantine regime is declared in the Zone. If you have fever, cough or difficulty breathing, stay at home and call your polyclinic or 103. Do not go to the polyclinic without calling first. Information line of the Ministry: +996-39-2-26-41-00 (8:00-22:00).
